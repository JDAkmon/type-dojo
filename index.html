Learn Pokémon type advantages, one matchup at a time.

Read the attacking move’s type first, then the defending Pokémon’s types. Water into Fire is 2×, while Fire into Water is ½×. For two defending types, multiply both interactions: Rock into Fire/Flying is 2 × 2 = 4×. An immunity always gives zero.
