Type Dojo is a Pokémon type-learning adventure for teens and adults, from your first Water-versus-Fire lesson to dual-type battle decisions.

A little practice, a useful picture

A Water-type move beats Fire because water puts out fire. Ground stops Electric because the attack is grounded. Type Dojo pairs the game rules with memorable stories, then asks you to apply them in practice.

The Ground Zero course introduces a few types at a time. Later chapters cover immunities and dual types: two weaknesses can make 4× damage, two resistances can make ¼×, and an immunity always makes the total zero.
